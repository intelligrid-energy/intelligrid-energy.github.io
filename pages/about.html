Laboratory Overview

Who we are

IntelliGrid Energy Dynamic is a research laboratory within the Department of Electrical Engineering at the National Institute of Technology Rourkela. The laboratory focuses on intelligent energy systems, smart grid technologies, power electronics, and the integration of renewable energy resources into modern power networks.

Our work spans fundamental research, applied engineering, and technology development, with the goal of contributing practical solutions to the challenges facing the modern electrical grid.

Research Objectives

What we aim to achieve

  • Advance intelligent control and automation for smart grids
  • Develop robust power electronic converters and control strategies
  • Enable reliable integration of renewable and distributed energy resources
  • Apply AI/ML techniques to forecasting, diagnostics, and grid optimization
  • Translate research into deployable technologies and prototypes

Mission

To conduct impactful research in intelligent energy systems and develop technologies that improve the efficiency, reliability, and sustainability of electrical power networks.

Vision

To be recognized as a center of excellence in smart grid and intelligent energy research, fostering innovation that bridges academic research and real-world energy infrastructure.

Research Philosophy

We believe the future grid must be intelligent, decentralized, and resilient — driven by data, robust control theory, and scalable engineering solutions.
